05, The part you're skeptical about

You've abandoned tools like this before.

So has everyone. Worth being honest about why, and what's different here.

I'll set it up and never open it again.

That happens when a tool asks you to maintain it. This one only ever shows you what's due today, and if nothing is due, it shows you nothing. There's no backlog glaring at you, no streak to break, nothing to feel guilty about on a quiet week.

This is going to feel fake and salesy.

Nothing sends itself. Not one message leaves without you writing it. The app never touches LinkedIn, never automates a word, and never speaks in your voice. It just makes sure you're the one who remembered.

I don't have time to enter a hundred people.

Then don't. Add the one person you just thought of and stop there. One name is a working system, and it takes about fifteen seconds. Most people add the second and third within a week, because the first one worked.

Isn't this just a spreadsheet with extra steps?

A spreadsheet needs you to remember to open it and work out who's overdue. That's the exact task you already fail at. The whole point is that the thinking happens without you.

The bit most tools hide

Finishing work gives your capacity back.

The cap counts active relationships, not everyone you've ever added. That distinction is worth more than the number itself.

Counts against your cap

  • Anyone mid-sequence
  • Anyone in conversation with you
  • Anyone on a nurture cadence
  • Communities you're tracking

Doesn't count, ever

  • Sequence complete
  • Marked not relevant
  • Archived
  • Opted out

Meaning a Pro plan isn't "150 people, then buy more." It's 150 at once, forever. Close out a raise and those relationships stop consuming capacity while staying fully in your records. Most people never outgrow the tier they start on.

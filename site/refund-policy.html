Refund Policy

If it isn't right, you get your money back.

Last updated: August 23, 2026

The short version

  • 14 days, full refund, no explanation needed. Email within 14 days of a payment and it gets refunded in full.
  • Cancel anytime from inside the app. Two clicks, no retention call.
  • Your data is never deleted on cancellation. You drop to the free tier and keep your records.
  • Annual plans get the same 14 days from the date of each renewal charge.

Everything below is the same policy stated more precisely. If the two ever seem to disagree, the detailed sections govern.

01 Who you're paying

GrowthSeeded uses Paddle.com Market Ltd as its Merchant of Record. When you buy a subscription, Paddle is the seller on the transaction, handles the payment, issues your invoice, and collects any applicable VAT or sales tax.

This matters for refunds in one practical way: refunds are returned through Paddle to the original payment method. Neither GrowthSeeded nor Paddle can send a refund to a different card, account, or person than the one that paid.

02 The 14-day refund

You may request a full refund of any subscription payment within 14 days of the date that payment was taken. You do not need to give a reason, and you will not be asked to complete a survey or take a call before it is processed.

This applies to:

  • Your first payment on a monthly or annual plan
  • Any subsequent renewal charge, counted from that renewal date
  • Payments made after upgrading between plans
Requesting a refund also cancels the subscription unless you say otherwise. If you want a refund but wish to keep using GrowthSeeded on the free tier, say so and nothing else changes.

03 How to request one

Email support@growthseeded.com from the address on the account, with the word "refund" somewhere in the message. Including the date of the payment helps but isn't required.

You'll get a reply confirming the request, normally within two business days. Once approved, Paddle typically returns the funds within five to ten business days, though the exact timing depends on your bank or card issuer rather than on us.

You can also request a refund directly through the Paddle receipt emailed to you at the time of purchase.

04 After 14 days

Beyond the 14-day window, payments are generally non-refundable, and a cancellation stops future charges rather than reversing past ones.

That said, this is a small product run by one person, not a policy department. If something genuinely went wrong, an outage, a charge you didn't intend, a renewal you tried to cancel and couldn't, get in touch. Cases like those are handled on their merits rather than by the calendar.

05 Cancelling, which is not the same thing

Cancelling stops the next charge. It does not refund the current period, and it does not need to: you keep full access until the period you've already paid for ends.

After that, the account moves to the free tier. Nothing you've recorded is deleted. Your relationships, notes, history, and sequences all remain; the free tier simply limits how many of them can stay active at once. Upgrading again restores full capacity immediately.

06 The free tier

The free tier involves no payment and therefore no refunds. It is not a time-limited trial, and it does not require a card. You are never automatically charged for using it.

07 Chargebacks

If you believe a charge is wrong, please email before disputing it with your bank. A refund request is faster than a chargeback, usually by several weeks, and it costs nothing.

Accounts with an open chargeback may be suspended while it is resolved, since Paddle reverses the transaction and the associated access along with it. Contacting us first avoids this entirely.

08 Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version appears on this page with a new date at the top. Changes are never applied retroactively: the policy in effect on the day you paid is the one that governs that payment.

Still unsure about something?

Ask before you buy rather than after. A question about whether GrowthSeeded fits your situation is welcome and gets an honest answer, including "probably not" where that's the truth.

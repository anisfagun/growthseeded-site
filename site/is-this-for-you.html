Worth saying plainly

And here's who it isn't for.

Better you find out now than after a payment.

  • Anyone who wants messages sent automatically. GrowthSeeded never sends anything. If that's the job you're hiring for, this is the wrong tool and an automation product will serve you better.
  • Anyone working a scraped or purchased list. There's no import from a data vendor, no enrichment, and the caps make volume outreach impractical on purpose.
  • Sales teams needing a shared CRM. Every account is fully isolated. There are no shared pipelines, no deal stages, no manager dashboards.
  • Anyone tracking fewer than about five people. Your memory is genuinely fine at that size. Come back when it isn't.

If you're in one of those groups, please don't sign up. A refund is easy but your time isn't, and a product that quietly takes money from people it can't help isn't one worth building.
